# Evidence

This directory contains the primary sources and supporting references used to verify the relationship between Donald Trump's disclosed investment transactions and U.S. policy events during 2025–2026.

The purpose of this section is to make every material claim in the dataset independently traceable.

## 2. Policy and Government Events

Whenever possible, policy events are verified using official government or company announcements.

### Intel — U.S. Government Investment

Intel and the Trump Administration announced an agreement under which the U.S. government would acquire a 9.9% stake in Intel.

Announcement date:

**August 22, 2025**

Official Intel announcement:

https://newsroom.intel.com/corporate/intel-and-trump-administration-reach-historic-agreement

This event is compared with disclosed purchases of Intel shares in Trump's investment accounts.

## 4. Counter-Evidence and Alternative Explanations

Evidence that weakens or provides an alternative explanation for a transaction-policy relationship is recorded with the same importance as evidence supporting the hypothesis.

### Intel — Public Information Before Purchase

Reuters reported on August 14, 2025 that the Trump administration was discussing the possibility of taking a stake in Intel.

Reuters:

https://www.reuters.com/world/china/trump-weighs-taking-stake-intel-bloomberg-news-reports-2025-08-14/

This report predates the August 18 Intel purchase identified in subsequent reporting.

Therefore, the Intel transaction cannot be treated simply as a purchase made before previously unknown government action.

Public information was already available.

This is an important counterexample and must remain part of the dataset.

## 5. Evidence Classification

Evidence used in this project is classified as:

### Primary

- OGE financial disclosure
- White House announcement
- Federal agency document
- Official company announcement concerning a government action

### Secondary — High Reliability

- Reuters
- Associated Press
- Wall Street Journal
- Other major financial or investigative reporting

### Research Lead

Information that identifies a potentially relevant transaction or event but still requires primary-source verification.

---

## Evidence Principle

The existence of a transaction before a policy event is not evidence by itself of illegal conduct.

The objective of this repository is to document:

**what was traded, when it was traded, what government action followed, what information was already public, and what can — and cannot — reasonably be concluded from the available evidence.**

Evidence that contradicts the original hypothesis is preserved rather than removed.
