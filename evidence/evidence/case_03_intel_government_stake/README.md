# Case 03 — Intel: Purchase Before the U.S. Government Stake Announcement

## Transaction

**Trade date:** August 18, 2025

**Company:** Intel Corporation

**Ticker:** INTC

**Transaction:** Purchase

**Reported amount:** At least $250,000

The Intel purchase occurred during a very large trading day across Trump's investment accounts.

Reporting based on the financial disclosure indicates that more than $75 million in securities were traded on August 18, making it one of the largest trading days of 2025.

Large purchases on the same day also included Nvidia, Apple, and Microsoft.

---

## Policy Event

On August 22, 2025, Intel and the Trump Administration formally announced an agreement under which the U.S. government would invest $8.9 billion in Intel common stock.

Under the agreement:

- The U.S. government agreed to acquire 433.3 million Intel shares.
- The purchase price was $20.47 per share.
- The government would hold approximately 9.9% of Intel.
- The investment included previously awarded CHIPS Act and Secure Enclave funding.

---

## Simple Temporal Relationship

Trade Date:

**August 18, 2025**

↓ 4 calendar days

Formal Government Agreement:

**August 22, 2025**

On this basis alone:

**Trade Before Formal Announcement = 4 days**

However, this timeline is incomplete.

---

## Prior Public Information

### August 11, 2025

Intel publicly confirmed that CEO Lip-Bu Tan had met President Trump.

Intel stated that the discussion concerned the company's commitment to strengthening U.S. technology and manufacturing leadership.

### August 14, 2025

Reuters reported that the Trump Administration was discussing the possibility of the U.S. government taking an equity stake in Intel.

The report cited an earlier Bloomberg report based on people familiar with the discussions.

Intel shares rose sharply following the news.

Therefore, the possibility of a U.S. government investment in Intel was public information before the August 18 transaction.

---

## Revised Information Timeline

**August 14, 2025**

Government stake possibility publicly reported

↓ 4 days

**August 18, 2025**

Intel shares purchased in Trump's investment accounts

↓ 4 days

**August 22, 2025**

Government-Intel agreement formally announced

---

## Additional Same-Day Information

August 18 was also significant for another reason.

Intel and SoftBank signed a separate agreement under which SoftBank would invest:

**$2 billion**

in Intel common stock at:

**$23 per share**

The SoftBank agreement was publicly announced on August 18.

This created an additional public market catalyst affecting Intel independently of the U.S. government's investment.

---

## Broader Portfolio Context

The Intel purchase was not an isolated transaction.

August 18 was one of the largest trading days of the year across Trump's investment accounts.

Reported purchases included substantial positions in:

- Nvidia
- Apple
- Microsoft
- Intel
- other large companies

This broader trading pattern must be considered when evaluating whether Intel was specifically targeted because of the forthcoming government action.

---

## What Is Verified

- Trump-related investment accounts purchased Intel shares on August 18, 2025.
- The reported purchase was at least $250,000.
- The formal U.S. government-Intel agreement was announced on August 22.
- The government obtained approximately 9.9% of Intel.
- Public reporting about a possible government stake existed by August 14.
- Intel's CEO had publicly confirmed a meeting with President Trump on August 11.
- SoftBank announced a separate $2 billion Intel investment on August 18.
- August 18 was a large portfolio-trading day rather than an Intel-only transaction.

---

## What Is Not Verified

The available public record does not establish:

- whether Trump's investment managers possessed information beyond what had already been publicly reported;
- whether they knew the final size, price, or structure of the government investment;
- whether the Intel purchase was motivated by the forthcoming government agreement;
- whether the investment managers communicated with government officials about Intel;
- whether the transaction differed from the accounts' normal portfolio-management strategy.

---

## Alternative Explanation

The August 18 Intel purchase can plausibly be explained using information already available to ordinary market participants.

By the time of the transaction:

- the Trump-Intel relationship was publicly known;
- a possible government equity investment had been reported;
- Intel shares had already reacted to that report;
- the accounts were simultaneously making large purchases across major technology companies;
- SoftBank made a separate $2 billion investment in Intel on the same date.

These facts substantially weaken any inference based solely on the four-day gap between the purchase and the formal government announcement.

---

## Current Assessment

**Evidence Status: VERIFIED**

**Trade Before Formal Announcement: YES — 4 days**

**Trade Before First Public Report: NO**

**Prior Public Information: STRONG**

**Portfolio-Specific Signal: WEAK TO MODERATE**

**Evidence of Illegal Trading: NOT ESTABLISHED**

---

## Research Significance

Case 03 demonstrates why the formal announcement date alone is not sufficient for evaluating transaction timing.

A trade may precede an official government announcement while still occurring after the material policy possibility has already entered the public information environment.

For this reason, future cases in this project should distinguish between:

**First Public Information Date**

and

**Official Announcement Date**
