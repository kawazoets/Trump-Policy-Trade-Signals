# Case 01 — April 8, 2025: 327 Stock Purchases Before the Tariff Pause

## Transaction

**Trade date:** April 8, 2025  
**Transaction type:** Purchase  
**Number of transactions:** 327  
**Reported minimum total:** More than $3.6 million  
**Sales identified on the date:** 0, according to WSJ analysis

Primary source:

U.S. Office of Government Ethics  
Donald J. Trump — 2025 Annual Financial Disclosure

The OGE filing contains exactly 327 transaction entries dated `4/8/2025`.

- numerous other large-, mid-, and small-cap securities

## Policy Timeline

### April 2, 2025

President Trump announced broad reciprocal tariffs.

Country-specific tariff rates were scheduled to take effect on April 9.

### April 8, 2025

Trump's disclosed investment accounts recorded 327 stock purchases.

### April 9, 2025

President Trump announced a 90-day pause on many of the country-specific reciprocal tariffs.

The policy change was later formalized through Executive Order 14266.

## Market Reaction

On April 9, 2025:

- S&P 500: +9.5%
- Nasdaq Composite: +12.16%
- Dow Jones Industrial Average: +7.82%

The rally followed the tariff-pause announcement.

## What Is Verified

- The 327 April 8 transactions appear in Trump's official OGE annual financial disclosure.
- All 327 were recorded as purchases.
- The tariff-pause announcement occurred the following day.
- The announcement materially moved U.S. equity markets.

## What Is Not Verified

The public record reviewed here does not establish:

- the precise execution times of the April 8 trades;
- who made each individual investment decision;
- whether the investment manager knew a tariff pause would be announced;
- whether material nonpublic information was communicated;
- whether the trades were motivated by the forthcoming policy decision.

Therefore, the timing alone does not establish insider trading or other illegal conduct.

---

## Alternative Explanation

The accounts conducted very large numbers of transactions throughout 2025.

Broad purchases across hundreds of securities may be consistent with automated portfolio management, direct indexing, rebalancing, tax management, or other systematic investment strategies.

This possibility must be tested against other trading days rather than treating April 8 in isolation.
