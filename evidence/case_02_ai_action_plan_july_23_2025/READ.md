# Case 02 — July 23, 2025: AI-Related Purchases on the Day of the AI Action Plan

## Transaction Date

**July 23, 2025**

Primary source:

U.S. Office of Government Ethics  
Donald J. Trump — 2025 Annual Financial Disclosure

---

## Total Trading Activity

The OGE disclosure shows 279 transactions in Investment Account #6 dated July 23, 2025.

- Purchases: 251
- Sales: 28
- Total transactions: 279

This is important because the six major AI-related purchases were not the only transactions made that day.

The account purchased securities across a broad range of sectors.

## Policy Event

On July 23, 2025, the White House released:

**Winning the AI Race: America's AI Action Plan**

The plan contained more than 90 Federal policy actions organized around three pillars:

1. Accelerating AI Innovation
2. Building American AI Infrastructure
3. Leading in International AI Diplomacy and Security

The plan included policies affecting AI infrastructure, data centers, semiconductor hardware, AI exports, Federal AI adoption, and regulation.

---

## Prior Public Information

The AI Action Plan itself was not an unexpected policy initiative.

On January 23, 2025, President Trump issued an Executive Order directing the Administration to develop an AI Action Plan within 180 days.

Therefore, the fact that a major AI policy plan would be developed during 2025 was already public information months before the July 23 transactions.

---

## Temporal Relationship

Trade Date:

**July 23, 2025**

Policy Release Date:

**July 23, 2025**

Temporal gap:

**0 calendar days**

However, the OGE disclosure does not provide transaction execution times.

The available public record therefore does not establish whether the purchases occurred before or after the AI Action Plan became public.

---

## What Is Verified

- 279 transactions were recorded in Investment Account #6 on July 23, 2025.
- 251 were purchases and 28 were sales.
- The six purchases in the highest reported transaction-value range were Broadcom, Meta, Amazon, Apple, Microsoft, and Nvidia.
- Each of those six purchases was reported in the $1,000,001–$5,000,000 range.
- The White House released America's AI Action Plan on July 23, 2025.
- The development of an AI Action Plan had been publicly ordered in January 2025.

---

## What Is Not Verified

The public evidence reviewed here does not establish:

- the precise execution time of any July 23 trade;
- whether the six largest purchases occurred before or after publication of the AI Action Plan;
- whether investment managers had access to nonpublic details of the final plan;
- whether the final policy provisions influenced the investment decisions.

---

## Alternative Explanation

The July 23 activity involved hundreds of securities across many sectors.

The transaction pattern may reflect:

- systematic portfolio rebalancing;
- direct indexing;
- broad-market allocation changes;
- automated investment management;
- changes based on already-public information.

The fact that unrelated securities were also purchased and sold is important counter-evidence against treating the six AI-related purchases in isolation.

---

## Research Significance

A notable feature remains:

**all six purchases in the highest disclosed transaction-value range on July 23 were major technology companies with substantial exposure to AI.**

This makes the day worth further examination, but it does not establish illegal trading.
